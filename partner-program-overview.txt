I AM REEL — PARTNER PROGRAM & WOMEN’S FUTURE-SELF PILOT
Proposed programs | October 2026

Meaningful partnerships. Brighter futures.
Personalized future-self imagery for counselors, coaches, retreat leaders and women-centered organizations.

PARTNERSHIP MODELS
Referral partner: Refer clients or participants seeking a personalized way to picture a brighter future.
Program partner: Explore co-branded future-self experiences for programs, retreats or events.
Pilot partner: Test the experience with a women’s group, community or membership program.
Organization partner: Explore a future private-label program as the platform develops.

THE PROPOSED PILOT
A focused guided experience with personalized digital imagery or a short visualization video; optional co-branding; feedback from partners and participants to refine the experience.
Pilot scope, pricing, consent and commercial terms will be agreed before participation. No commission structure, licensing terms or formal partner commitments are represented as established.

PREPARING A PARTNERSHIP CONVERSATION
Consider your organization and audience, your preferred partnership model, your program goals, a suitable pilot format, participant consent and review needs, and how you would gather feedback.

RESPONSIBLE USE
Participants choose their own goals and approve their imagery. The experience supports reflection, education and personal growth. It is not therapy, medical care or a substitute for professional treatment.

CURRENT STATUS
Personalized cinematic production has been demonstrated manually. One spiritual counselor has expressed interest in using the service with clients. The integrated automated platform, professional workflows and pilot partner program remain in development.

DEVELOPMENT PRIORITIES
Secure intake; production tools and reusable workflows; pilot outreach and feedback; samples; planned commerce and print-on-demand integrations.
