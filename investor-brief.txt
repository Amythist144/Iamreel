THE SCIENCE BEHIND SEEING YOUR FUTURE
Research-informed visualization, designed to connect possibility with action.

Rösch et al. (2022): 48 articles, 174 effect sizes; episodic future thinking shifted intertemporal decisions toward delayed rewards (g=.44), with stronger effects for positive, vivid events relevant to personal goals.

Ye et al. (2022): 47 studies, 63 contrasts; reduced delay discounting (g=.52); positive future imagery subgroup g=.64. https://doi.org/10.1177/17470218211066282

Wang et al. (2021): 21 studies, 24 independent effect sizes, 15,907 participants; combining mental contrasting with implementation intentions improved goal attainment (g=.336). Publication bias may influence confidence in this estimate. The systematic review is relevant to implementation planning, but it is not a direct test of cinematic visualization.

These are standardized effects, not percentage improvements. The two future-thinking reviews cover related evidence and should not be added together as independent totals. These studies evaluate future-thinking and planning exercises; they do not establish that AI-generated cinematic experiences will produce equivalent outcomes.

THE OPPORTUNITY
I AM REEL is developing an AI visualization engine that transforms a person's goal and reference photos into personalized cinematic future experiences. A manually produced proof of concept demonstrates the emotional appeal and market potential. The next phase is automating the experience, quality control, and repeatable delivery.

ONE ENGINE, THREE APPLICATIONS
Personal: My Future — individual goals, relationships, career, travel, home and life transitions.
Professional: Their Future — proposed workflows for counselors, coaches, educators and organizations helping clients picture personally chosen goals.
Global: Our Future — the Global Vision Project, a proposed collective positive-future storytelling application.

COMPANION PRODUCTS
