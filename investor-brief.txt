THE SCIENCE BEHIND SEEING YOUR FUTURE
Research-informed visualization, designed to connect possibility with action.

Rösch et al. (2022): 48 articles, 174 effect sizes; episodic future thinking shifted intertemporal decisions toward delayed rewards (g=.44), with stronger effects for positive, vivid events relevant to the choice. https://doi.org/10.1037/xge0001148

Ye et al. (2022): 47 studies, 63 contrasts; reduced delay discounting (g=.52); positive future imagery subgroup g=.64. https://doi.org/10.1177/17470218211066282

Wang et al. (2021): 21 studies, 24 independent effect sizes, 15,907 participants; combining mental contrasting with implementation intentions improved goal attainment (g=.336). Publication bias may inflate the estimate. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.565202

These are standardized effects, not percentage improvements. The two future-thinking reviews cover related evidence and should not be added together as independent totals. These studies evaluate future-thinking and planning exercises, not I AM REEL videos. Product-specific and lasting benefits remain to be evaluated.

THE OPPORTUNITY
I AM REEL is developing an AI visualization engine that transforms a person's goal and reference photos into personalized cinematic future experiences. A manually produced proof of concept demonstrates the intended output. The next step is automating the production workflow into secure, repeatable software.

ONE ENGINE, THREE APPLICATIONS
Personal: My Future — individual goals, relationships, career, travel, home and life transitions.
Professional: Their Future — proposed workflows for counselors, coaches, educators and organizations helping clients picture personally chosen goals.
Global: Our Future — the Global Vision Project, a proposed collective positive-future storytelling application.

COMPANION PRODUCTS
Approved vision imagery can become affirmation cards, wallpapers, framed art, pillows, blankets, journals, candles, yoga mats and other print-on-demand products.
