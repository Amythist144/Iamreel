02 / The scientific foundation

The Science Behind
Seeing Your Future.

Research on vividly imagining future possibilities suggests it can help people choose longer-term rewards and act on higher-value goals.

48

articles · 174 effect sizes

Imagining specific future experiences shifted decisions toward delayed rewards, with a medium effect (g = .44).

47

studies · 63 comparisons

Future-thinking exercises reduced preference for immediate over delayed rewards (g = .52).

15,907

participants · 21 studies

Combining mental contrasting with implementation intentions improved goal attainment (g = .336).

These are standardized effect sizes, not percentage improvements. They support the concept that vivid future imagery can help orient attention and effort toward meaningful goals.
