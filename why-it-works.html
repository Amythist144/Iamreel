The scientific foundation

The Science Behind
Seeing Your Future.

Research on vividly imagining specific future experiences supports a promising foundation for visualization connected to real goals, obstacles, and action.

48

articles · 174 effect sizes

Imagining specific future experiences shifted decisions toward delayed rewards, with a medium effect (g = .44). Effects were stronger when events were positive, vivid, and related to the choice.

Rösch et al., 2022 · Read study
47

studies · 63 comparisons

Future-thinking exercises reduced preference for immediate over delayed rewards (g = .52). Positive future imagery showed a larger effect (g = .64).

Ye et al., 2022 · Read study
15,907

participants · 21 studies

Contrasting a desired future with present obstacles and making specific if–then action plans improved goal attainment, with a small-to-medium effect (g = .336).

Wang et al., 2021 · Read study

These are standardized effect sizes, not percentage improvements. The two future-thinking reviews cover related research and should not be counted as entirely separate bodies of evidence.

Evidence for the approach. Evaluation for the product.

The studies evaluate guided future-thinking and planning exercises. They do not establish that watching I AM REEL’s AI-generated videos produces the same effects. Product-specific benefits, repeated viewing, and lasting outcomes remain to be tested through pilots.
