I AM REEL Founder and Visionary: Sheri Berry

If we want to see a different world outside of ourselves, we first have to envision it within.

I Created I AM REEL because so much of what we see in the media paints a dark future for humanity. My mission is to change that by creating images of a positive future we can believe in.

I believe we have to shape our inner vision first, before we will see it mirrored in the outside world. Seeing those pictures vividly and repeatedly changes how we see ourselves, and can then change what we see outside of ourselves.

That is the importance of I AM REEL: personalized cinematic visualizations that allow you to actually see yourself living the life and future you want to create.

But beyond our individual lives lies a greater vision for all of Humanity.

Through the I AM REEL Global Vision Project, I want to ask people everywhere: What do you want the future of humanity to look like?

We'll collect those visions and ultimately bring them together into a cinematic vision of our shared future—not based on what we fear might happen, but on what we actually want for our world.

Helping humanity envision a brighter future,
one person at a time.
